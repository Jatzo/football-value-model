An accumulator multiplies the odds, and the model multiplies its chances, which is fair because the legs come from different matches. Two bets on the same match are linked, so the slip takes one leg per match. Small edges compound in an accumulator, but so do the model's errors: in the backtests single bets chosen by edge already lost money, and an accumulator of them would lose faster.
